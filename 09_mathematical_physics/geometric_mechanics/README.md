# Geometric mechanics bridge

A configuration space $Q$ with metric $g$ induces kinetic energy

$$
T(q,\dot q)=\frac12 g_q(\dot q,\dot q).
$$

With no potential and affine parameterization, extremizing

$$
S[\gamma]=\frac12\int g_{ij}(q)\dot q^i\dot q^j\,dt
$$

produces the geodesic equation

$$
\ddot q^k+\Gamma^k{}_{ij}\dot q^i\dot q^j=0.
$$

This gives the flagship computational example a mathematical-physics interpretation: the same connection coefficients that describe intrinsic geometry also appear in free motion on the manifold.

For engineering use, do not assume a resilience trajectory is a geodesic. That interpretation requires a defensible metric/action and must be tested against the governing physical/operational dynamics.
